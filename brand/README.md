# brand/

Logos and fonts for the title and end card.

- `oufp.png`, `garden-steward.png`: transparent PNGs in cream, for the dark
  overlay. Footage Studio's *Title & end card* settings pick these by name.
  `oufp-dark.png` is the original black OUFP logo, for light backgrounds.
- `fonts/`: the caption fonts (Playfair Display and its italic, EB Garamond
  italic, Cormorant Garamond italic, Courier Prime, Roboto, Caveat), all from
  Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License (each one's `OFL-*.txt` beside it). The title and
  end card use Playfair Display and Roboto from here too, and fall back to
  Georgia and Helvetica without them.
